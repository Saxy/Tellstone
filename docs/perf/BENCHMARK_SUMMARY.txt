=== Phase 1 Benchmark Summary ===

After completing Phase 1 optimizations, we ran comprehensive benchmarks to measure the impact of our changes. Here's a summary of the key findings:

1. **Get Operation (BenchmarkCoreGet)**:
   - Before: 22.45 - 23.13 ns/op, 0 B/op, 0 allocs/op
   - After: 22.46 - 22.97 ns/op, 0 B/op, 0 allocs/op
   - Analysis: No significant change in performance. This is expected as the Get operation was already optimized and meeting the Phase 1 exit criteria (0 allocs/op).

2. **Set Operation (BenchmarkCoreSet)**:
   - Before: 67.72 - 68.41 ns/op, 64 B/op, 1 allocs/op
   - After: 68.07 - 69.05 ns/op, 64 B/op, 1 allocs/op
   - Analysis: No significant change in performance. The 1 allocation per operation is structural and cannot be eliminated without changing the ownership model.

3. **Batch Operation (BenchmarkCoreBatch)**:
   - Before: 481.2 - 496.2 ns/op, 64-65 B/op, 1 allocs/op
   - After: 480.1 - 495.4 ns/op, 64-65 B/op, 1 allocs/op
   - Analysis: No significant change in performance. The 1 allocation per operation is structural and cannot be eliminated without changing the ownership model.

**Conclusion**:
- Phase 1 optimizations have been successfully implemented and documented.
- The core storage engine was already meeting the Phase 1 exit criteria:
  - `BenchmarkKVGet` -> 0 allocs/op (already satisfied)
  - `BenchmarkKVSet` -> 1 alloc/op (structural, not fixable without changing ownership model)
- No significant performance improvements were observed in the benchmarks because the optimizations we implemented (W4 and W5) were focused on the router and persistence layers, which are not covered by these core benchmarks.
- The Phase 1 optimizations have been completed and documented.
